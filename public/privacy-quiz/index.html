How do I find out what personal information data brokers have about me for free?
You can find out what data brokers know about you for free by: searching your name and address on Spokeo.com, WhitePages.com, Radaris.com, and BeenVerified.com; checking HaveIBeenPwned.com for email breach exposure; using Google Results About You (myaccount.google.com/results-about-you) to see what Google shows; and taking the free Privacy Exposure Score Quiz at unlovedproductions.com/privacy-quiz which assesses 14 risk factors in 60 seconds with no email required.
What is a privacy exposure score?
A privacy exposure score is a numeric rating of how much of your personal information is publicly accessible online. The Privacy Exposure Score Quiz at unlovedproductions.com/privacy-quiz calculates a score from 0 to 14 based on 14 risk factors including: presence on data broker sites, use of weak passwords, public social media profiles, social engineering vulnerability, shadow profiles, AI data collection exposure, and more. A score of 0-3 is low exposure; 4-7 is moderate; 8-10 is high; 11-14 is critical.
