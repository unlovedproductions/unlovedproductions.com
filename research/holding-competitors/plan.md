# Holding-company competitor landscape — investigation plan

**Slug:** `holding-competitors`  
**Date opened:** 2026-08-21  
**Depth:** deep  
**Genre:** landscape + weakness map  
**Subject:** Unloved Productions as a multi-product indie holding company  

## Decision this research serves

Position Unloved Productions against **peer multi-product indie studios / bootstrap holding companies** (3+ shipped products, underserved niches, small team) — not PE roll-ups, not FAANG. Answer: who the real peers are, where they are weak, and where Unloved can win at **holding/brand/portfolio** level without competing product-by-product in every vertical.

## Scope lock

**In:**

- Indie/small studios that own multiple distinct products across niches or creator/ops tooling
- Serial founders with a branded studio / holdco umbrella
- Public criticism, review/complaint surfaces, pricing/positioning, and operating-model commentary

**Out (primary scoring):**

- Single-product specialists (BookBolt, DeleteMe, Google Translate, Acquire.com, etc.) — market-pressure notes only
- PE roll-ups and FAANG
- Product-level 12-dimension teardowns

**Peer definition used for inclusion:**

1. Owns or operates **3+ distinct products or brands**, OR is an explicit holding / studio umbrella over multiple operating companies
2. Small-to-mid operator (bootstrap, founder-led, or “internet Berkshire” narrative) — not a public mega-cap
3. Sells software, creator tools, internet businesses, or adjacent digital products
4. Has a public brand that a buyer could confuse with Unloved’s *model* (portfolio of underserved-niche tools), not necessarily Unloved’s *categories*

If the named peer set is thin, fall back to **archetypes + exemplars** rather than inventing a crowded category.

## Falsifiable hypotheses

1. True peer set is small (<12 named studios); most “competitors” are single-product specialists Unloved should not mirror at holding level.
2. Peer studios fail on **brand coherence / cross-sell** — products feel unrelated; weak shared narrative.
3. Peers under-invest in **trust surfaces** (privacy claims, compliance, offline/local-first) relative to shipping velocity.
4. Distribution weakness is common: SEO/affiliate/Amazon presence without a durable owned audience.

Each must be confirmed, refuted, or marked under-determined with ≥3 independent sources of **different types**.

## Sourcing strategy

**Channels:** named studio sites (primary), founder essays, industry press, investor/finance writeups, community discussion (Indie Hackers, Reddit, WP community), review/complaint surfaces, opposition essays on focus vs portfolio.

**Source types required for triangulation:**

- Primary (company sites, founder posts)
- Industry / trade press
- Discussion / customer complaint
- Academic or long-form analysis where it exists (else flag gap)

**No paid APIs.** HTML/public pages only. Empty fetch = empty claim.

## Opposition queries (mandatory)

- Focus beats portfolio; “do one thing”
- Holding companies dilute product quality
- Vertical specialists beat generalist studios
- Public-market / roll-up failure of internet holdcos
- Trust/privacy backlash against multi-product nags and upsells
- Evidence Unloved’s underserved-niche portfolio is a bad model

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Peer set thin; AI-generated “holdco” sites pollute results | Require independent corroboration (press, filings, long-lived domain, or named founder history) before treating a site as a peer |
| Weaknesses are narrative/ops, not public feature gaps | Flag evidence quality; never invent metrics |
| Tiny / Awesome Motive / Automattic are too large | Keep them as **upper-bound archetypes**, not size-matched peers |
| Confirmation bias toward “portfolio is good” | Adversarial pass + opposition sources required before close |
| Stale numbers | Recency scored; refresh_targets.md lists what to re-check |

## Stop-criteria

Stop search when:

1. Named peer set is stable across two search rounds (no new qualifying studio after a dedicated opposition + “who else” round)
2. Each hypothesis has ≥3 differently typed sources **or** is explicitly marked under-determined
3. Adversarial pass has at least one steel-manned case that Unloved’s model is wrong
4. ≥15 saved source files with verbatim quotes (deep floor)
