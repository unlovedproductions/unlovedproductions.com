# F2 — Brand coherence fails unless products share a customer

**Hypothesis 2:** Peer studios fail on brand coherence / cross-sell — products feel unrelated; weak shared narrative.

**Verdict:** Confirmed, with a split.

**Claim:** Cross-sell and a believable parent story only work when products share an ICP *or* a famous founder. Unrelated portfolios do not cross-sell; they stack. Same-ecosystem portfolios cross-sell hard and pay a honesty tax.

## Cluster A — Unrelated products, personal brand as glue

Levels and Marc Lou do not sell “the Levels suite.” They sell through a person. Marc Lou’s most famous product is not his top earner ([21](../sources/21_medium_marc_lou_graveyard.md)). VDL admits 2–3 products get real attention and the rest sit in maintenance ([18](../sources/18_vdl_portfolio.md)).

Jason Fried: people knew Basecamp, not 37signals — which is why they renamed the company ([04](../sources/04_jason_fried_bos.md)).

## Cluster B — Same customer, hidden parent

Awesome Motive brands products separately on purpose. Searchers do not know WPForms and MonsterInsights are siblings ([07](../sources/07_ellipsis_am_marketing.md)). Cross-sell is SEO occupancy and in-admin nags, not a holding-company story. Founder admits outsiders think they are an auto shop ([06](../sources/06_syed_balkhi_am_manual.md)).

## Cluster C — Coherent studio, expensive second product

37signals rebuilt the umbrella brand so Basecamp + HEY + more could coexist ([01](../sources/01_37signals_hello_again.md)). Doist puts Todoist and Twist under one philosophy; Twist still lagged (~$600K vs ~$14M) ([16](../sources/16_firstmrr_doist.md)) and the founder says they were too early ([17](../sources/17_linkedin_amir_twist.md)). Buffer’s Reply served a different department and died ([05](../sources/05_buffer_sunset_reply.md)).

Tiny and saas.group **choose** incoherence: decentralized operating companies, no integration playbook ([25](../sources/25_tiny_homepage.md), [14](../sources/14_productled_saas_group.md)).

**Implication for Unloved:** KDP tools, Ghost Protocol, ReviveHQ, Puente, and Stoic books do not share an ICP. Parent-brand cross-sell will not work like AM. The honest analog is Levels/VDL (stack + graveyard risk), not WPForms→OptinMonster.

**Types:** primary + industry + discussion.
