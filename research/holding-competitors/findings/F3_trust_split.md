# F3 — Trust surfaces: split, not a blanket under-investment

**Hypothesis 3:** Peers under-invest in trust surfaces (privacy, compliance, offline/local-first) relative to shipping velocity.

**Verdict:** Refuted as a blanket. Confirmed as a cluster for growth-machine portfolios.

**Claim:** Peers split into trust-first, growth-first, and ship-first. Unloved should not assume “everyone neglects trust.” Some over-invest in it (37signals). Some burn it for installs (Awesome Motive). Ship-first studios mostly do not talk about it at all — evidence there is thin, so that sub-claim is under-determined.

## Trust-first (disconfirming H3)

> We don’t sell customer data to anyone … Our business model is selling products, not selling you. ([02](../sources/02_37signals_we_dont_sell_you.md))

Paid product, no ad targeting, manifesto-level. This is an over-investment relative to shipping slogans.

## Growth-first (confirming for this cluster)

AM: admin nags, countdown upsells, tutorial-as-placement ([08](../sources/08_wp_minute_am_problem.md), [09](../sources/09_wp_tavern_sandhills.md)). August 2026: WPForms Lite onboarding sent admins offsite with a one-hour token and forced extra plugin installs; “Privacy Compliance” checkbox could not be unticked in SEJ’s test ([10](../sources/10_sej_wpforms.md), [11](../sources/11_webmastered_wpforms.md)). Community pushback: not a NIST backdoor, still opaque consent. Irony: a “privacy compliance” install path that users cannot refuse.

## Ship-first (under-determined)

VDL lists security patches as maintenance cost, not a differentiator ([18](../sources/18_vdl_portfolio.md)). Levels/Marc Lou coverage is about shipping and audience, not privacy claims ([19](../sources/19_solopreneurship_levels.md), [20](../sources/20_solopreneurship_marc_lou.md)). Absence of discussion ≠ under-investment. Do not overclaim.

**Implication:** Ghost Protocol and Puente (offline) are trust-native products. The holding-level gap vs AM is a positioning weapon *if* Unloved does not copy AM’s onboarding opacity. Vs 37signals, Unloved is behind on a paid-product trust manifesto at the parent brand.

**Types:** primary + industry + discussion. Academic/legal analysis of the WPForms token is missing; SEJ is the strongest independent test.
