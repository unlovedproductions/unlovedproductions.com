# F5 — The second-product tax is the load-bearing weakness

**Claim:** Across bootstrap studios, the second (or nth) product is where quality, focus, and GTM break. This is the shared weakness Unloved is most exposed to.

**Evidence:**

1. **Jason Fried (2020):** six products ≈ 18 surfaces (web/iOS/Android); 20–30 people could not meet their own quality bar ([04](../sources/04_jason_fried_bos.md)). They focused, then later re-expanded only after HEY was a hit ([01](../sources/01_37signals_hello_again.md)).
2. **Amir Salihefendic:** Twist “too early” at ~30 people while Todoist was growing; “complete ruin” is on the table; only go multi-product if growth stalls ([17](../sources/17_linkedin_amir_twist.md)). Twist ~$600K vs Todoist ~$14M in the FirstMRR recap ([16](../sources/16_firstmrr_doist.md)).
3. **Joel Gascoigne:** Reply = 500 customers vs Buffer 70,000; different department; different sales motion; slow fixes; sunset ([05](../sources/05_buffer_sunset_reply.md)).
4. **VDL (2026):** sublinear attention; 8 products = 2–3 loved, rest neglected; customers of the neglected products lose to focused competitors ([18](../sources/18_vdl_portfolio.md)).

**Steel-man against this finding:** Founder Reality and 37signals 2022 argue the *season* changed — AI makes single-product moats weak, so portfolios are defensive ([23](../sources/23_founder_reality_seasons.md), [01](../sources/01_37signals_hello_again.md)). That is an argument for *adjacent* products to a working customer, not for five unrelated markets.

**Unloved exposure:** Five live/coming product lines plus an imprint, one operator, unrelated ICPs. That is VDL’s tax without VDL’s shared Next.js/Stripe stack across all of them (Flutter/Flask KDP suite vs privacy quiz vs deal intel vs Android translator vs KDP paperbacks).

**Types:** primary + industry + discussion.
