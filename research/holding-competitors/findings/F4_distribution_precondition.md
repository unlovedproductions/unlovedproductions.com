# F4 — Distribution without a durable owned audience is the default failure

**Hypothesis 4:** Distribution weakness is common: SEO/affiliate/Amazon presence without a durable owned audience.

**Verdict:** Confirmed for ship-first copycats; refined for SEO machines.

**Claim:** Multi-product only compounds after distribution is solved. Three distribution types showed up. Unloved currently has none of them at peer scale.

## Type 1 — Founder-owned audience

Levels: “The code is not the moat. The audience is.” Without it, “launch ten things” yields ten half-finished zeros ([19](../sources/19_solopreneurship_levels.md)). Marc Lou: revenue posts *are* the marketing ([20](../sources/20_solopreneurship_marc_lou.md)). Graveyard of 39 / 70+ listed products underneath the winners ([21](../sources/21_medium_marc_lou_graveyard.md)).

## Type 2 — Content/search inventory (not a community)

AM: ~1.75M monthly visits across sites in the Ellipsis snapshot; WPBeginner plus product blogs occupy multiple top-10 slots for revenue keywords; readers do not know the sites are siblings ([07](../sources/07_ellipsis_am_marketing.md)). Medeiros: tutorial product-placement is the brand strategy ([08](../sources/08_wp_minute_am_problem.md)). This *is* durable distribution — it is just not an “owned audience” in the Levels sense. H4’s wording is slightly too narrow: SEO can be durable; it is still not a relationship you can email without buying more content.

## Type 3 — Idea-led company brand

37signals: books, Signal essays, “we don’t sell you.” Founder Reality (weaker source) argues founder trust is the post-AI moat and that you should still not diversify in “winter” ([23](../sources/23_founder_reality_seasons.md)). Buffer had 70k paying customers on the core product and still could not transfer that audience to Reply ([05](../sources/05_buffer_sunset_reply.md)).

**Implication:** Unloved’s site reads as a holding company with five products and no WPBeginner, no Nomad List community, and no *Rework*-scale idea engine. Shipping more products will not fix that. Copying AM SEO without a single ecosystem (WordPress) will not replicate AM’s network effect.

**Types:** primary + industry + discussion.
