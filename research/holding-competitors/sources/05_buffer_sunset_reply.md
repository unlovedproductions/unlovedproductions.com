## Verbatim quotes

> We originally acquired it intending to diversify Buffer’s core product offerings. While it served that purpose, it has become clear over time that Reply is better suited for larger organizations and is not the best product for Buffer’s core customer base, small businesses.

> Often the people using Reply were very different from those using our other Buffer solutions. They usually weren’t even in the same department at a given company.

> We’re a team of 89 people at Buffer, and we don’t have the resources to run two very different strategies.

> We have been slow to fix issues and add new features, and we saw how this negatively affected our customer’s team’s operations.

> To make this more tangible, we had only 500 customers using Reply, while we have over 70,000 paying customers using Buffer.

## Notes

Documented second-product failure: different ICP, different GTM motion, quality decay, then sunset. Direct analog to Unloved shipping unrelated verticals.
