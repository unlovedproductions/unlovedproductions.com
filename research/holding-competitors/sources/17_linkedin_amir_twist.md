## Verbatim quotes

Christophe Pasquier: He was very clear: "I wouldn't do it. Focus on Slite, on what works. You'll lose focus and kill your momentum." … they have built 2 incredible products, Todoist and Twist. They made Twist work, it's a terrific product, but it took them much more effort and focus than originally expected. And of course all the time spent there distracted from their core offering.

Amir Salihefendic: Becoming a multi-product company is a critical decision that can take you to new heights or complete ruin. In Twist’s case, I think we were too early (we were about 30 people, and Todoist was growing like crazy). In hindsight, I would only consider multi product if growth stalls. If you have something growing like crazy, I would triple down on that instead of spreading ficus.
