## Verbatim quotes

> Levels runs a portfolio of internet products, all as a solo operator with no co-founder and no team. The best-known are: Nomad List … RemoteOK … PhotoAI …

> He has publicly stated this portfolio generates over $3 million a year in revenue, run entirely by one person.

> Own the distribution. This is the part newcomers underrate. Before PhotoAI, before most of his products, Levels had spent years building an audience … When he ships something, he has a channel to put it in front of people on day one. The code is not the moat. The audience is.

> The portfolio approach can become a trap. Running many products works because he already owns distribution to a single audience. Without that, “launch ten things” usually produces ten half-finished projects at zero revenue … Levels is not evidence that scattering bets works; he’s evidence that scattering bets works once you’ve already solved distribution.

## Notes

Revenue is self-reported, unaudited. The transferable claim is the distribution precondition, not the $3M figure.
